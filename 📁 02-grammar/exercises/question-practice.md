## Exercise 12: Conversation Questions

**Complete the conversation with appropriate questions:**

A: Hi! _____?
B: I'm fine, thanks! And you?

A: Great! _____ on weekends?
B: I usually go to the park or watch movies.

A: Nice! _____ to the movies?
B: About once a month.

A: _____ of movies do you like?
B: I love action movies and comedies.

A: Me too! _____ last movie you watched?
B: I watched "Fast X" last week.

A: _____ was it?
B: It was amazing! Very exciting!

A: _____ with you?
B: I went with my brother.

A: Cool! _____ this weekend?
B: I'm going to visit my grandparents.

A: _____ do they live?
B: They live in the countryside, about two hours from here.

A: That sounds nice! _____ back?
B: I'll be back on Sunday evening.
