## Exercise 10: Reading Comprehension with Grammar Focus

**Read and answer:**

My name is Maria and I am a student. I study at City University. I have been studying there for two years. Every day, I wake up at 7 AM and I go to university by bus. The bus ride takes about 30 minutes.

This semester, I am taking five classes. My favorite class is English. I study English because I want to travel to the United States next year. I have never been there before, but I have always wanted to visit New York.

Last week, I took an English test. While I was taking the test, I was very nervous. The test was difficult, but I think I did well. I will get my results next week.

Right now, I am sitting in the library. I am writing an essay for my history class. Some of my friends are studying here too. After I finish my essay, I will meet them for lunch.

**Questions:**

1. How long has Maria been studying at City University?
2. What time does she wake up?
3. How does she go to university?
4. How many classes is she taking this semester?
5. Why does she study English?
6. Has she been to the United States before?
7. When did she take an English test?
8. How did she feel during the test?
9. What is she doing right now?
10. What will she do after finishing her essay?
