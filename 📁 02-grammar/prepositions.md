# 🗺️ Prepositions

## What are Prepositions?

Prepositions are small words that show the relationship between things. They usually come before nouns or pronouns.

**Common prepositions:** in, on, at, to, from, by, with, about, for, of, etc.

---

## 1️⃣ Prepositions of TIME

### AT - Specific Time

**Use AT for:**
- **Clock times:** at 3 o'clock, at 7:30, at noon, at midnight
- **Specific moments:** at night, at sunrise, at sunset
- **Holidays (no day):** at Christmas, at Easter
- **Meal times:** at breakfast, at lunch, at dinner

### Examples
✅ "The meeting is **at** 9 AM."
✅ "I wake up **at** 6:30."
✅ "We eat **at** noon."
✅ "They arrived **at** midnight."
✅ "**At** night, I read books."
✅ "We visit family **at** Christmas."

---

### ON - Days and Dates

**Use ON for:**
- **Days:** on Monday, on Friday
- **Dates:** on January 5th, on the 20th
- **Specific days:** on my birthday, on New Year's Day
- **Days + parts:** on Monday morning, on Friday night

### Examples
✅ "I have class **on** Tuesday."
✅ "My birthday is **on** June 15th."
✅ "The party is **on** Saturday night."
✅ "She was born **on** December 25th."
✅ "We meet **on** the first Monday of every month."

---

### IN - Longer Periods

**Use IN for:**
- **Months:** in January, in July
- **Years:** in 2025, in 1990
- **Seasons:** in summer, in winter
- **Centuries:** in the 21st century
- **Parts of day:** in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening
- **Future time:** in 2 hours, in a week, in 5 minutes

### Examples
✅ "I was born **in** 1995."
✅ "School starts **in** September."
✅ "It's hot **in** summer."
✅ "I exercise **in** the morning."
✅ "She'll arrive **in** 30 minutes."
✅ "We lived there **in** the 1990s."

---

### 🎯 AT vs ON vs IN Summary

| AT | ON | IN |
|----|----|----|
| at 7 PM | on Monday | in January |
| at noon | on July 4th | in 2025 |
| at midnight | on my birthday | in summer |
| at night | on Friday evening | in the morning |
| at Christmas | on New Year's Day | in the afternoon |

### ⚠️ Special Cases - No Preposition!

**Don't use prepositions with:**
- **yesterday, today, tomorrow**
  - ✅ "I saw him **yesterday**." (not "on yesterday")
  - ✅ "She's coming **tomorrow**." (not "in tomorrow")

- **this/that/next/last + time**
  - ✅ "**This** Monday" (not "on this Monday")
  - ✅ "**Next** week" (not "in next week")
  - ✅ "**Last** year" (not "in last year")

## 2️⃣ Prepositions of PLACE

### AT - Specific Point/Location

**Use AT for:**
- **Specific addresses:** at 123 Main Street
- **Specific locations:** at the bus stop, at the door
- **Events:** at a party, at a concert, at a meeting
- **Buildings (as point):** at the store, at the bank

### Examples
✅ "I'm **at** home."
✅ "She's **at** work."
✅ "Meet me **at** the coffee shop."
✅ "I saw her **at** the party."
✅ "He's waiting **at** the bus stop."

---

### ON - Surface/Contact

**Use ON for:**
- **Surfaces:** on the table, on the wall, on the floor
- **Streets:** on Main Street
- **Transportation (riding):** on the bus, on the train, on a plane
- **Devices:** on TV, on the radio, on the phone
- **Floors:** on the 5th floor

### Examples
✅ "The book is **on** the table."
✅ "Put it **on** the shelf."
✅ "I live **on** Oak Street."
✅ "I saw it **on** TV."
✅ "She's **on** the bus right now."
✅ "My office is **on** the third floor."

---

### IN - Enclosed/Inside

**Use IN for:**
- **Cities/countries:** in New York, in Brazil
- **Enclosed spaces:** in the room, in the car, in the box
- **Large areas:** in the park, in the sky, in the ocean
- **Buildings (inside):** in the hospital, in the office
- **Publications:** in a book, in a magazine, in the newspaper

### Examples
✅ "I live **in** Boston."
✅ "She's **in** the kitchen."
✅ "The keys are **in** my bag."
✅ "There are clouds **in** the sky."
✅ "He's **in** the car."
✅ "I read it **in** the newspaper."

---

### 🎯 AT vs ON vs IN (Place) Summary

| AT (point) | ON (surface) | IN (enclosed) |
|------------|--------------|---------------|
| at the door | on the wall | in the room |
| at the corner | on the floor | in the building |
| at the bus stop | on the table | in the car |
| at home | on Main Street | in New York |
| at the party | on the bus | in the box |

## 3️⃣ Prepositions of MOVEMENT

### TO - Direction/Destination
- "I'm going **to** work."
- "She walked **to** the store."
- "Come **to** my house."

### FROM - Origin/Starting Point
- "I'm **from** Brazil."
- "She's coming **from** work."
- "This gift is **from** my mother."

### INTO - Entering
- "He walked **into** the room."
- "She jumped **into** the pool."
- "Put it **into** the box."

### OUT OF - Exiting
- "He came **out of** the house."
- "Take it **out of** the bag."
- "She got **out of** the car."

### ACROSS - From one side to the other
- "She walked **across** the street."
- "The bridge goes **across** the river."

### THROUGH - Moving inside from one end to the other
- "We drove **through** the tunnel."
- "He walked **through** the park."

### ALONG - Following a line/path
- "Walk **along** the river."
- "Drive **along** this street."

### PAST - Going by
- "She walked **past** the store."
- "The bus went **past** my stop."

## 4️⃣ Other Important Prepositions

### BY - Method/Means/Near
- "I go to work **by** car."
- "Send it **by** email."
- "She was standing **by** the window."
- "The house is **by** the lake."

### WITH - Accompaniment/Using
- "I went **with** my friends."
- "Cut it **with** a knife."
- "Coffee **with** milk."

### WITHOUT - Absence
- "I can't live **without** music."
- "She came **without** her husband."

### FOR - Purpose/Duration/Recipient
- "This is **for** you." (recipient)
- "I studied **for** 3 hours." (duration)
- "This knife is **for** cutting bread." (purpose)

### ABOUT - Topic/Subject
- "We talked **about** the weather."
- "What's this book **about**?"
- "I'm thinking **about** you."

### OF - Possession/Part of whole
- "The color **of** the car."
- "A piece **of** cake."
- "The president **of** the company."

### BETWEEN - Two things
- "It's **between** you and me."
- "The bank is **between** the store and the café."

### AMONG - More than two things
- "She was **among** friends."
- "Divide it **among** all students."

## 5️⃣ Prepositions with Transportation

### Use IN:
- **in** a car
- **in** a taxi
- **in** a truck

### Use ON:
- **on** a bus
- **on** a train
- **on** a plane
- **on** a ship
- **on** a bike
- **on** a motorcycle

### Use BY (method):
- **by** car
- **by** bus
- **by** train
- **by** plane
- **by** bike

### But:
- **on** foot (walking)

### Examples
✅ "I go to work **by** car."
✅ "She's **in** the car right now."
✅ "He's **on** the bus."
✅ "I travel **by** plane."
✅ "We went there **on** foot."

## 6️⃣ Common Prepositional Phrases

### Time Expressions
- **in time** = early enough
- **on time** = at the scheduled time
- **at times** = sometimes
- **for a while** = for a short period
- **in a moment** = very soon

### Location Expressions
- **at the top/bottom**
- **in front of** ↔ **behind**
- **next to** = beside
- **in the middle of**
- **at the beginning/end**

### Other Common Phrases
- **good at** (something)
- **interested in**
- **afraid of**
- **famous for**
- **different from**
- **married to**
- **tired of**
- **angry with** (person) / **angry about** (situation)

## 7️⃣ Common Mistakes ⚠️

### Wrong Preposition

❌ "I live **in** Main Street."
✅ "I live **on** Main Street."

❌ "She arrived **in** 5 o'clock."
✅ "She arrived **at** 5 o'clock."

❌ "I saw him **in** Monday."
✅ "I saw him **on** Monday."

❌ "I go to work **in** bus."
✅ "I go to work **by** bus."

❌ "I'm waiting you."
✅ "I'm waiting **for** you."

❌ "I arrived **to** home."
✅ "I arrived **at** home." / "I arrived home." (no preposition)

### Missing Preposition

❌ "I'm listening music."
✅ "I'm listening **to** music."

❌ "I'll see you Monday."
✅ "I'll see you **on** Monday."

## 🎯 Remember!

**Time:**
- **AT** = specific times
- **ON** = days and dates
- **IN** = longer periods

**Place:**
- **AT** = specific point
- **ON** = surface
- **IN** = enclosed space

**Movement:**
- **TO** = destination
- **FROM** = origin
- **INTO/OUT OF** = entering/exiting

Prepositions are tricky, but practice makes perfect! 🎯
