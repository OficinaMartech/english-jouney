# ❓ Question Formation

## 1️⃣ Yes/No Questions

### Structure with BE (am/is/are/was/were)

**Formula:** Be + Subject + ...?

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| You are a student. | Are you a student? |
| She is happy. | Is she happy? |
| They were here. | Were they here? |
| I am late. | Am I late? |

### Structure with DO/DOES/DID

**Present Simple:**
**Formula:** Do/Does + Subject + base verb + ...?

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| You work here. | Do you work here? |
| She likes coffee. | Does she like coffee? |
| They play soccer. | Do they play soccer? |

**Past Simple:**
**Formula:** Did + Subject + base verb + ...?

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| You worked yesterday. | Did you work yesterday? |
| She went home. | Did she go home? |
| They studied. | Did they study? |

### ⚠️ Important Rules!

1. **With DO/DOES/DID, use BASE VERB** (no -s, no -ed)
   - ❌ "Does she likes...?"
   - ✅ "Does she like...?"
   - ❌ "Did they went...?"
   - ✅ "Did they go...?"

2. **Don't use DO with BE**
   - ❌ "Do you are a student?"
   - ✅ "Are you a student?"

3. **Don't use DO with CAN/WILL/COULD/SHOULD**
   - ❌ "Do you can swim?"
   - ✅ "Can you swim?"

## 2️⃣ WH- Questions (Information Questions)

### The WH- Words

| Word | Asks About | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **What** | Things/Actions | What is your name? |
| **Where** | Places | Where do you live? |
| **When** | Time | When is your birthday? |
| **Who** | People | Who is your teacher? |
| **Why** | Reasons | Why are you late? |
| **How** | Manner/Method | How do you go to work? |
| **Which** | Choice | Which color do you prefer? |
| **Whose** | Possession | Whose book is this? |

### Structure

**Formula:** WH-word + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb + ...?

**With BE:**
- What **is** your name?
- Where **are** you from?
- When **was** the meeting?

**With DO/DOES/DID:**
- What do you **do**? (job)
- Where does she **live**?
- When did they **arrive**?

**With WILL:**
- What will you **do**?
- When will she **come**?

**With CAN:**
- What can I **do**?
- Where can we **go**?

## 3️⃣ Special Cases: WHO and WHAT as Subject

### When WHO/WHAT is the SUBJECT

**No DO/DOES/DID needed!**

**Formula:** Who/What + verb + ...?

### Examples

**Who as subject:**
- "**Who** called you?" (not "Who did call you?")
- "**Who** lives here?"
- "**Who** took my pen?"

**What as subject:**
- "**What** happened?"
- "**What** made that noise?"
- "**What** causes rain?"

### Compare:

| Who = Subject (no do) | Who = Object (use do) |
|------------------------|------------------------|
| Who called you? | Who did you call? |
| Who broke the window? | Who did you see? |
| Who helps you? | Who do you help? |

## 4️⃣ HOW Questions

### Basic HOW
- "**How** are you?" (feeling)
- "**How** do you make this?" (method)
- "**How** did you come here?" (method)

### HOW + Adjective/Adverb

| Question | Asks About | Example Answer |
|----------|------------|----------------|
| How **old** | Age | I'm 25 years old. |
| How **tall** | Height | I'm 6 feet tall. |
| How **long** | Duration/Length | 2 hours / 5 meters |
| How **far** | Distance | 10 miles / 5 kilometers |
| How **much** | Price/Quantity | $20 / A lot |
| How **many** | Number (countable) | 5 books / 10 people |
| How **often** | Frequency | Every day / Once a week |
| How **big** | Size | Very big / Small |
| How **deep** | Depth | 10 feet deep |

### Examples
- "**How old** is your brother?" - "He's 18."
- "**How much** does it cost?" - "It costs $50."
- "**How many** children do you have?" - "I have two."
- "**How often** do you exercise?" - "Three times a week."
- "**How long** is the movie?" - "It's two hours long."
- "**How far** is the beach?" - "It's about 5 miles."

## 5️⃣ Question Words + TO + Infinitive

### Common Patterns
- **How to** + verb: "I don't know **how to swim**."
- **What to** + verb: "I don't know **what to do**."
- **Where to** + verb: "Do you know **where to go**?"
- **When to** + verb: "Tell me **when to start**."
- **Who to** + verb: "I don't know **who to ask**."

### Examples
✅ "Can you show me **how to cook** this?"
✅ "I don't know **what to say**."
✅ "Do you know **where to buy** tickets?"
✅ "She doesn't know **who to trust**."

## 6️⃣ Tag Questions

### What are Tag Questions?
Short questions added at the end of statements to confirm or check information.

### Basic Rule
**Positive statement → Negative tag**
**Negative statement → Positive tag**

### Examples

| Statement | Tag | Full Sentence |
|-----------|-----|---------------|
| You are John, | aren't you? | You are John, aren't you? |
| She likes coffee, | doesn't she? | She likes coffee, doesn't she? |
| They went home, | didn't they? | They went home, didn't they? |
| You aren't tired, | are you? | You aren't tired, are you? |
| He doesn't work here, | does he? | He doesn't work here, does he? |
| You can swim, | can't you? | You can swim, can't you? |

### Common Tag Questions
- "Nice day, **isn't it**?"
- "You like pizza, **don't you**?"
- "She didn't call, **did she**?"
- "They will come, **won't they**?"
- "You can help me, **can't you**?"

## 📝 Quick Practice

**Make questions:**

1. She lives in New York. → Where _______?
2. They went to the beach. → Where _______?
3. He is 25 years old. → How old _______?
4. The movie starts at 7 PM. → When _______?
5. John called you. → Who _______?
6. It costs $50. → How much _______?
7. You are a teacher. → _______, aren't you?

**Answers:**
1. Where does she live?
2. Where did they go?
3. How old is he?
4. When does the movie start?
5. Who called you? (no auxiliary!)
6. How much does it cost?
7. You are a teacher, aren't you?

---

## 🎯 Remember!

- **Yes/No Questions:** Use auxiliary (do/does/did/can/will) + subject + base verb
- **BE is special:** Just invert BE and subject
- **WH- Questions:** WH-word + auxiliary + subject + verb
- **Who/What as subject:** No auxiliary needed!
- **How much** vs **How many:** much = uncountable, many = countable
- **Tag questions:** Positive → negative tag / Negative → positive tag

Keep asking great questions! 🎯
