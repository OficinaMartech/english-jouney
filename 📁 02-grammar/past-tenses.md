# ⏪ Past Tenses

## 1️⃣ Simple Past

### When to Use
- Completed actions in the past: "I walked to school yesterday."
- Past habits: "She studied every night."
- Historical facts: "World War II ended in 1945."

### Structure

**Regular Verbs:** Add **-ed**
**Irregular Verbs:** Special forms (see list below)

### Regular Verbs - Adding -ED

**Most verbs:** add -ed
- work → work**ed**
- play → play**ed**
- watch → watch**ed**

**Verbs ending in -e:** add -d only
- live → liv**ed**
- love → lov**ed**
- use → us**ed**

**Verbs ending in consonant + y:** change y to -ied
- study → stud**ied**
- try → tr**ied**
- carry → carr**ied**

**Short verbs (CVC):** double last letter + -ed
- stop → stop**ped**
- plan → plan**ned**
- prefer → prefer**red**

- yesterday
- last (night/week/month/year)
- ago (two days ago, a week ago)
- in 2020, in the past
- when I was young

### Examples
✅ "I **worked** yesterday."
✅ "She **went** to the store last week."
✅ "They **studied** English two years ago."
✅ "We **had** a great time at the party."

## 2️⃣ Past Continuous (Past Progressive)

### When to Use
- Action in progress at a specific time in the past: "I was studying at 8 PM."
- Background action interrupted: "I was cooking when she called."
- Two actions happening simultaneously: "While I was reading, he was watching TV."

### Structure

**Formula:** Subject + was/were + verb + **-ing**

| Subject | Was/Were | Verb-ing | Example |
|---------|----------|----------|---------|
| I | was | studying | I was studying |
| You | were | working | You were working |
| He/She/It | was | eating | She was eating |
| We | were | watching | We were watching |
| They | were | playing | They were playing |

### 💡 Common Patterns

**Action interrupted by another:**
- "I **was sleeping** when the phone **rang**."
- "She **was driving** when she **saw** the accident."

**Two actions at the same time:**
- "While I **was cooking**, he **was cleaning**."
- "They **were studying** while we **were watching** TV."

**Specific time in the past:**
- "At 7 PM yesterday, I **was having** dinner."
- "This time last year, we **were living** in Texas."

### Examples
✅ "I **was watching** TV at 9 PM yesterday."
✅ "What **were** you **doing** when I called?"
✅ "While she **was reading**, I **was cooking**."
✅ "They **were playing** soccer when it started to rain."

---

## 🆚 Simple Past vs Past Continuous

### Simple Past = Completed Action
- "I **studied** English yesterday." (finished action)
- "He **called** me." (one complete action)

### Past Continuous = Action in Progress
- "I **was studying** English at 8 PM." (in progress at specific time)
- "He **was calling** me when I arrived." (ongoing action)

### Together in One Sentence

**When + Simple Past, Past Continuous**
- "I **was taking** a shower when someone **knocked** on the door."
- "She **was driving** when she **heard** the news."

**While + Past Continuous, Past Continuous**
- "While I **was studying**, my brother **was sleeping**."
- "While they **were talking**, I **was listening** to music."

## 3️⃣ Used To (Past Habits)

### When to Use
- Past habits that no longer happen
- Past situations that are no longer true

### Structure

**Formula:** Subject + used to + base verb

### Examples
✅ "I **used to play** soccer every weekend." (but not anymore)
✅ "She **used to live** in California." (now she lives elsewhere)
✅ "They **used to be** friends." (but not now)
✅ "We **used to watch** TV every night." (past habit)

### vs Simple Past

**Used to** = repeated action/habit in past (no longer true)
- "I used to smoke." (I don't smoke now)

**Simple Past** = specific completed action
- "I smoked a cigarette yesterday." (one specific time)

## 📝 Quick Practice

**Choose Simple Past or Past Continuous:**

1. I _______ (walk) to school when I _______ (see) my friend.
2. What _______ you _______ (do) at 6 PM yesterday?
3. She _______ (cook) while he _______ (watch) TV.
4. They _______ (play) soccer when it _______ (start) to rain.
5. I _______ (use to / lived) in Brazil, but now I live in the US.

**Answers:**
1. was walking / saw
2. were / doing
3. was cooking / was watching
4. were playing / started
5. used to live

## 🎯 Remember!

- **Simple Past** = completed actions
- **Past Continuous** = actions in progress in the past
- **Used to** = past habits/situations (no longer true)
- Regular verbs add **-ed**
- Irregular verbs have special forms - memorize them!
- Use **was/were + -ing** for Past Continuous
