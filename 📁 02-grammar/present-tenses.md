# ⏰ Present Tenses

## 1️⃣ Simple Present

### When to Use
- Habits and routines: "I wake up at 7 AM."
- Facts and truths: "The sun rises in the east."
- Permanent situations: "She lives in New York."

### Structure

**Positive:**
- I/You/We/They + verb
- He/She/It + verb + **s/es**

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I | work | I work every day |
| You | work | You work hard |
| He/She/It | work**s** | She work**s** here |
| We | work | We work together |
| They | work | They work downtown |

### ⚠️ Special Cases - Adding S/ES/IES

**Add -S:**
- talk → talks
- eat → eats
- play → plays

**Add -ES (after -ch, -sh, -s, -x, -z, -o):**
- watch → watch**es**
- wash → wash**es**
- go → go**es**
- fix → fix**es**

**Change Y to IES (consonant + y):**
- study → stud**ies**
- try → tr**ies**
- fly → fl**ies**

**Keep Y + S (vowel + y):**
- play → plays
- say → says
- enjoy → enjoys

### 💡 Common Time Expressions
- every day/week/month/year
- always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never
- on Mondays, in the morning, at night

### Examples
✅ "I drink coffee every morning."
✅ "He studies English on Mondays."
✅ "They watch TV at night."
✅ "The store opens at 9 AM."

## 2️⃣ Present Continuous (Present Progressive)

### When to Use
- Actions happening NOW: "I am studying right now."
- Temporary situations: "She is living with friends this month."
- Future plans: "We are meeting tomorrow at 3."

### Structure

**Formula:** Subject + am/is/are + verb + **-ing**

| Subject | Be | Verb-ing | Example |
|---------|-----|----------|---------|
| I | am | studying | I am studying now |
| You | are | working | You are working |
| He/She/It | is | eating | She is eating lunch |
| We | are | watching | We are watching TV |
| They | are | playing | They are playing soccer |

### How to Add -ING

**Most verbs:** add -ing
- work → working
- eat → eating
- study → studying

**Verbs ending in -e:** remove e, add -ing
- write → writ**ing**
- make → mak**ing**
- come → com**ing**

**Short verbs (CVC - consonant/vowel/consonant):** double last letter
- run → run**n**ing
- sit → sit**t**ing
- swim → swim**m**ing
- stop → stop**p**ing

**⚠️ Exception:** Don't double w, x, y
- snow → snowing
- fix → fixing
- play → playing

### 💡 Common Time Expressions
- now, right now, at the moment
- today, this week, this month
- Look! Listen! (to draw attention)

### Examples
✅ "I am learning English now."
✅ "Look! It is raining!"
✅ "They are watching a movie at the moment."
✅ "She is staying with us this week."

---

## 🆚 Simple Present vs Present Continuous

### Simple Present = Routine/Habit
- "I **work** from 9 to 5." (my schedule)
- "He **plays** soccer every Saturday." (regular habit)

### Present Continuous = Now/Temporary
- "I **am working** on a project right now." (at this moment)
- "He **is playing** soccer in the park." (happening now)

### Side by Side Comparison

| Simple Present | Present Continuous |
|----------------|-------------------|
| She lives in Boston. (permanent) | She is living in Boston this year. (temporary) |
| I work at a bank. (my job) | I am working on a report. (right now) |
| They study English. (general) | They are studying for a test. (at the moment) |

---

## ⚠️ Stative Verbs (No -ing Usually!)

These verbs describe **states**, not actions. We usually don't use them in continuous form:

**Mental States:**
- know, understand, believe, think (opinion), remember, forget

**Emotions:**
- love, like, hate, prefer, want, need

**Senses:**
- see, hear, smell, taste, feel (involuntary)

**Possession:**
- have (possess), own, belong

**Being:**
- be, seem, appear

### Examples
❌ "I am knowing the answer." 
✅ "I know the answer."

❌ "She is loving chocolate."
✅ "She loves chocolate."

❌ "They are having a car."
✅ "They have a car."

## 📝 Quick Practice

**Fill in the blanks with Simple Present or Present Continuous:**

1. I usually _______ (drink) coffee, but today I _______ (drink) tea.
2. She _______ (study) English every day. Right now, she _______ (study) grammar.
3. Look! The baby _______ (sleep).
4. My brother _______ (work) at a hospital. He _______ (save) lives.
5. They _______ (watch) TV at the moment, but they usually _______ (read) books.

**Answers:**
1. drink / am drinking
2. studies / is studying
3. is sleeping
4. works / saves
5. are watching / read

## 🎯 Remember!

- **Simple Present** = habits, facts, routines
- **Present Continuous** = now, temporary
- Add **-s/-es/-ies** to he/she/it in Simple Present
- Add **-ing** for Present Continuous
- Don't use -ing with stative verbs!

Keep practicing - you're building great habits! 🌟
