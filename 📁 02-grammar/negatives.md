# ❌ Negative Forms

## 1️⃣ Negative with BE (am/is/are/was/were)

### Structure
**Formula:** Subject + BE + not + ...

### Present

| Full Form | Contraction 1 | Contraction 2 |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| I am not | I'm not | ❌ |
| You are not | You're not | You aren't |
| He is not | He's not | He isn't |
| She is not | She's not | She isn't |
| It is not | It's not | It isn't |
| We are not | We're not | We aren't |
| They are not | They're not | They aren't |

### Past

| Full Form | Contraction |
|-----------|-------------|
| I was not | I wasn't |
| You were not | You weren't |
| He/She/It was not | wasn't |
| We were not | We weren't |
| They were not | They weren't |

### Examples
✅ "I **am not** tired."
✅ "She **isn't** here."
✅ "They **weren't** at home yesterday."
✅ "It **wasn't** expensive."

---

## 2️⃣ Negative with DO/DOES/DID

### Present Simple Negative

**Formula:** Subject + do/does + not + base verb

| Subject | Auxiliary | Not | Base Verb | Contraction |
|---------|-----------|-----|-----------|-------------|
| I/You/We/They | do | not | work | don't work |
| He/She/It | does | not | work | doesn't work |

### ⚠️ Important!
**Use BASE VERB after don't/doesn't** (no -s, no -ed)

❌ "She doesn't works."
✅ "She doesn't work."

❌ "He don't like coffee."
✅ "He doesn't like coffee."

### Examples
✅ "I **don't** like vegetables."
✅ "She **doesn't** speak French."
✅ "They **don't** have a car."
✅ "He **doesn't** work on Sundays."

---

### Past Simple Negative

**Formula:** Subject + did + not + base verb

**Contraction:** didn't

### ⚠️ Use BASE VERB (not past form!)

❌ "I didn't went."
✅ "I didn't go."

❌ "She didn't worked."
✅ "She didn't work."

### Examples
✅ "I **didn't** go to school yesterday."
✅ "She **didn't** call me."
✅ "They **didn't** finish the project."
✅ "He **didn't** see the movie."

---

## 3️⃣ Negative with Modal Verbs

### Common Modals: CAN, WILL, WOULD, SHOULD, COULD, MUST

**Formula:** Subject + Modal + not + base verb

| Modal | Negative Full | Contraction |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| can not | cannot / can not | can't |
| will not | will not | won't |
| would not | would not | wouldn't |
| should not | should not | shouldn't |
| could not | could not | couldn't |
| must not | must not | mustn't |

### Examples
✅ "I **can't** swim."
✅ "She **won't** come to the party."
✅ "They **shouldn't** do that."
✅ "He **couldn't** find his keys."
✅ "You **mustn't** smoke here." (prohibited)

### Special: DON'T HAVE TO vs MUSTN'T

**Don't have to** = not necessary
- "You **don't have to** come." (optional, your choice)

**Mustn't** = prohibited, not allowed
- "You **mustn't** smoke here." (it's forbidden)

## 4️⃣ Negative with HAVE

### HAVE as Main Verb (possession)

**Two ways:**

**American English (more common):**
- "I **don't have** a car."
- "She **doesn't have** time."

**British English:**
- "I **haven't** a car." (less common)
- "She **hasn't** time." (less common)

**Recommendation:** Use don't/doesn't have (American style)

### HAVE as Auxiliary (Present Perfect)

**Formula:** Subject + have/has + not + past participle

| Full Form | Contraction |
|-----------|-------------|
| have not | haven't |
| has not | hasn't |

### Examples
✅ "I **haven't seen** that movie."
✅ "She **hasn't finished** yet."
✅ "They **haven't arrived**."

## 5️⃣ Negative Questions

### Structure
**Formula:** Auxiliary + n't + subject + verb + ...?

### Examples

| Positive Question | Negative Question |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Do you like coffee? | **Don't** you like coffee? |
| Does she work here? | **Doesn't** she work here? |
| Did they call? | **Didn't** they call? |
| Can you swim? | **Can't** you swim? |
| Is he coming? | **Isn't** he coming? |

### When to Use Negative Questions

**To show surprise:**
- "**Didn't** you know that?" (I thought you knew!)
- "**Can't** she drive?" (I'm surprised!)

**To check/confirm:**
- "**Don't** you like this?" (checking)
- "**Isn't** this your book?" (confirming)

**To make suggestions (polite):**
- "**Won't** you sit down?" (polite invitation)
- "**Wouldn't** you like some coffee?" (offer)

## 6️⃣ Double Negatives ⚠️

### ❌ Don't Use Double Negatives in English!

In English, two negatives make a positive. Unlike Portuguese, Spanish, or other languages, you should NOT use double negatives.

### Common Mistakes

❌ "I don't have nothing."
✅ "I don't have anything."
✅ "I have nothing."

❌ "She doesn't know nobody."
✅ "She doesn't know anybody."
✅ "She knows nobody."

❌ "They didn't go nowhere."
✅ "They didn't go anywhere."
✅ "They went nowhere."

❌ "He doesn't never call."
✅ "He doesn't ever call."
✅ "He never calls."

### Negative Words
- nothing, nobody/no one, nowhere, never, neither

### ANY Words (for negatives)
- anything, anybody/anyone, anywhere, ever

### Rule:
**Negative verb + ANY word**
OR
**Positive verb + NEGATIVE word**

## 7️⃣ NEITHER and NOR

### Neither = Nor I / Nor does he (agreement with negative)

**Pattern:** Neither + auxiliary + subject

### Examples
- A: "I don't like coffee."
- B: "**Neither do I**." (Me neither / I don't either)

- A: "She can't swim."
- B: "**Neither can I**."

- A: "They didn't go."
- B: "**Neither did we**."

### Informal: "Me neither" / "Me either"
- A: "I don't have a car."
- B: "Me neither!" (very informal, common in speech)

## 8️⃣ Negative Adverbs: Position

### Common Negative Adverbs
- never, rarely, seldom, hardly, barely, scarcely

### ⚠️ Don't use NOT with these!

❌ "I don't never smoke."
✅ "I never smoke."

❌ "She doesn't rarely call."
✅ "She rarely calls."

### Examples
✅ "I **never** eat meat."
✅ "She **rarely** goes out."
✅ "They **hardly** ever visit."
✅ "He **seldom** complains."

## 📝 Quick Practice

**Correct the mistakes:**

1. She don't like pizza.
2. I didn't went to school.
3. They doesn't have a dog.
4. I don't have nothing.
5. He can't never come on time.
6. We wasn't at home.
7. She don't never calls me.

**Answers:**
1. She **doesn't** like pizza.
2. I didn't **go** to school.
3. They **don't** have a dog.
4. I don't have **anything**. / I have **nothing**.
5. He **can never** come on time. / He **can't ever** come on time.
6. We **weren't** at home.
7. She **never** calls me. / She **doesn't ever** call me.

## 🎯 Remember!

- **BE:** subject + be + not
- **DO/DOES/DID:** subject + auxiliary + not + BASE VERB
- **Modals:** subject + modal + not + base verb
- **No double negatives!** Use "any" words with negative verbs
- **Never use -s or -ed after don't/doesn't/didn't**
- **Can't, won't, shouldn't** = most common contractions

You're getting the hang of negatives! Keep practicing! 💪
