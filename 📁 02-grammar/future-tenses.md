# ⏩ Future Tenses

## 1️⃣ Simple Future with WILL

### When to Use
- Predictions: "It will rain tomorrow."
- Spontaneous decisions: "I'm hungry. I'll make a sandwich."
- Promises: "I will help you."
- Offers: "I'll carry that for you."

### Structure

**Formula:** Subject + will + base verb

| Subject | Will | Base Verb | Example |
|---------|------|-----------|---------|
| I | will | go | I will go |
| You | will | study | You will study |
| He/She/It | will | work | She will work |
| We | will | eat | We will eat |
| They | will | come | They will come |

### 💡 Contractions
- I will = I'll
- You will = You'll
- He will = He'll
- She will = She'll
- It will = It'll
- We will = We'll
- They will = They'll

### Examples
✅ "I **will call** you tomorrow."
✅ "She **will be** 30 next month."
✅ "They **will arrive** at 8 PM."
✅ "It **will be** sunny tomorrow."

### Common Time Expressions
- tomorrow
- next (week/month/year)
- in the future
- soon, later
- in + time (in 2 hours, in 3 days)

## 2️⃣ Going To Future

### When to Use
- Plans already decided: "I'm going to visit my parents this weekend."
- Predictions based on evidence: "Look at those clouds! It's going to rain."
- Intentions: "I'm going to learn Spanish next year."

### Structure

**Formula:** Subject + am/is/are + going to + base verb

| Subject | Be | Going To | Base Verb | Example |
|---------|-----|----------|-----------|---------|
| I | am | going to | travel | I'm going to travel |
| You | are | going to | study | You're going to study |
| He/She/It | is | going to | work | She's going to work |
| We | are | going to | move | We're going to move |
| They | are | going to | buy | They're going to buy |

### Examples
✅ "I'm **going to study** tonight." (plan)
✅ "She's **going to buy** a new car." (decision already made)
✅ "Look! He's **going to fall**!" (evidence-based prediction)
✅ "They're **going to visit** us next month." (arranged plan)

---

## 🆚 WILL vs GOING TO

### Use WILL for:
**Spontaneous decisions (at the moment of speaking):**
- "The phone is ringing. I'll answer it."
- "I'm thirsty. I'll get some water."

**Predictions without evidence:**
- "I think it will be hot tomorrow."
- "She will probably be late."

**Promises/Offers:**
- "I'll help you with that."
- "Don't worry, I'll be there."

### Use GOING TO for:
**Plans already decided:**
- "I'm going to visit my parents." (already planned)
- "We're going to watch a movie tonight." (decided before)

**Predictions with evidence:**
- "Look at those clouds! It's going to rain." (I can see the clouds)
- "Be careful! You're going to fall!" (I see danger)

### Side by Side

| Situation | Will | Going To |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Phone rings | "I'll answer it!" (spontaneous) | ❌ |
| Weekend plans | ❌ | "I'm going to visit friends." (planned) |
| Dark clouds | ❌ | "It's going to rain." (evidence) |
| Offer help | "I'll help you!" | ❌ |

## 3️⃣ Present Continuous for Future Plans

### When to Use
- Fixed arrangements with specific time/place: "I'm meeting John at 3 PM."
- Scheduled personal plans: "We're flying to Miami on Friday."

### Structure

**Formula:** Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing + time expression

### Examples
✅ "I'm **having** dinner with my boss tonight." (arranged)
✅ "She's **flying** to London tomorrow." (ticket booked)
✅ "We're **meeting** at the coffee shop at 2 PM." (agreed time/place)
✅ "They're **leaving** next Monday." (scheduled)

### ⚠️ Must have time/place details!
✅ "I'm meeting Tom **tomorrow at 5**."
❌ "I'm meeting Tom someday." (too vague - use going to)

---

## 4️⃣ Simple Present for Timetables

### When to Use
- Fixed schedules (trains, buses, flights, movies, events)
- Official timetables

### Examples
✅ "The train **leaves** at 9:30 AM."
✅ "The movie **starts** at 7 PM."
✅ "School **begins** on September 1st."
✅ "The store **closes** at 10 PM."

---

## 🎯 Comparison Chart

| Form | Use | Example | Time Expression |
|------|-----|---------|-----------------|
| **WILL** | Spontaneous decision | "I'll call her now." | tomorrow, soon, later |
| **WILL** | Prediction (no evidence) | "It will rain." | next week, in the future |
| **GOING TO** | Plan already decided | "I'm going to study." | tonight, this weekend |
| **GOING TO** | Prediction (with evidence) | "It's going to rain!" | soon, any minute |
| **Present Continuous** | Fixed arrangement | "I'm meeting Tom at 5." | tomorrow at 3, tonight |
| **Simple Present** | Timetable | "The bus leaves at 8." | scheduled times |

---

## 5️⃣ Future Time Clauses

### ⚠️ Important Rule!
After these words, use **SIMPLE PRESENT** (not will) for future:
- when, before, after, until, as soon as, while, if

### Examples
✅ "When I **get** home, I'll call you." (not "will get")
✅ "I'll wait until she **arrives**." (not "will arrive")
✅ "If it **rains**, we'll stay home." (not "will rain")
✅ "As soon as I **finish**, I'll help you." (not "will finish")

### Pattern:
**When/If + Simple Present, Subject + WILL + verb**

## 📝 Quick Practice

**Choose the correct form (will / going to / present continuous / simple present):**

1. "I'm tired. I think I _______ (go) to bed early tonight."
2. "Look at those dark clouds! It _______ (rain)."
3. "I _______ (meet) Sarah at the café tomorrow at 3 PM."
4. "The train _______ (leave) at 10:30 AM every morning."
5. "We have plans. We _______ (visit) our grandparents this weekend."
6. "When you _______ (arrive), call me."
7. A: "We don't have milk." B: "Don't worry. I _______ (buy) some."

**Answers:**
1. will go (spontaneous decision)
2. is going to rain (evidence)
3. am meeting (fixed arrangement)
4. leaves (timetable)
5. are going to visit (plan)
6. arrive (time clause - use simple present)
7. will buy (spontaneous decision)

## 🎯 Remember!

- **WILL** = spontaneous decisions, predictions without evidence
- **GOING TO** = plans already made, predictions with evidence
- **Present Continuous** = fixed arrangements (with time/place)
- **Simple Present** = timetables and schedules
- After when/if/before/after/until = use **Simple Present** for future!
